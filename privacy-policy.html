Privacy Policy for AI SponsorBlock for YouTube™

Last Updated: March 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how AI SponsorBlock for YouTube™ ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our browser extension on Microsoft Edge and Chromium browsers.

1. Core Principle: Privacy by Design

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not track your browsing history or personal web activity.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data to third parties.
  • We do not require an account, email address, password, or login to use the extension.

2. What Information We Access and Why

To detect and skip sponsorships within YouTube videos, the extension accesses the following technical data:

  • Public YouTube Video Identifier & Captions: The extension extracts the public YouTube Video ID and public closed caption cues/transcript directly from the player. These captions are sent to our AI analysis backend to pinpoint sponsorship timestamps. Results are cached in Supabase PostgreSQL so future viewers don't need to re-scan.
  • Local Extension Storage (chrome.storage.local): Stores your personal extension toggles (e.g. auto-skip enabled, skip self-promos) and local aggregate counters (number of sponsors skipped, seconds saved). This data resides exclusively in your browser profile.
  • Community Feedback (Optional): If you click "Undo & Report", an anonymous report is submitted to Supabase to adjust community confidence and suppress bad detections.

4. Third-Party Services

When analyzing video transcripts, the extension interacts strictly with encrypted HTTPS APIs:

  • Supabase: For cloud caching of video timestamps.
  • Groq Cloud API: For AI inference using Qwen 3.8 27B model on public video transcripts.
  • SponsorBlock API: For fallback community segment lookups.
