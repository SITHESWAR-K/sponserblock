Privacy Policy for AI SponsorBlock for YouTube™

Last Updated: March 2025 • Policy Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how AI SponsorBlock for YouTube™ ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our browser extension and visit our web portal at sponsorblockyt.web.app.

1. Core Privacy Principle

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not track your personal browsing history, search history, or general web activity.
  • We do not monetize, sell, rent, or trade any user data to advertising networks, analytics brokers, or data brokers.
  • We do not require an account, email address, password, or login to use the extension.

2. Technical Data Processed by the Extension

To detect and skip sponsorships inside YouTube videos, the extension accesses the following technical data:

  • Public YouTube Video Identifier & Captions: The extension extracts the public YouTube Video ID and public closed caption cues/transcript directly from the YouTube HTML5 player. These captions are processed by our Groq AI backend to pinpoint sponsorship timestamps. Results are cached in Supabase PostgreSQL so subsequent viewers don't need to re-scan.
  • Local Extension Storage (chrome.storage.local): Stores user preferences (e.g. auto-skip enabled, skip self-promos) and local aggregate counters (number of sponsors skipped, seconds saved). This data resides exclusively in your browser profile on your device.
  • Community Feedback (Optional): If you click "Undo & Report", an anonymous report is submitted to Supabase to adjust community confidence and suppress bad detections.

4. Third-Party Services

When analyzing video transcripts, the extension interacts strictly with encrypted HTTPS APIs:

  • Supabase: For cloud caching of video timestamps and community votes.
  • Groq Cloud API: For AI inference using Qwen 3.8 27B model on public video transcripts.
  • SponsorBlock API: For fallback community segment lookups.

5. Contact & Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the developer.
