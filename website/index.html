The Cold-Start Problem

Why traditional crowdsourcing leaves you exposed

Community-driven databases work well for viral videos from three months ago. They fail completely on the videos you actually watch every day.

Traditional SponsorBlock

100% Volunteer Dependent

A human being has to watch the video, open the submission editor, find the exact frame where the sponsor starts, and click submit.

  • ✕ First 2-6 hours are unprotected: You are guaranteed to hear the sponsor pitch on freshly uploaded content.
  • ✕ Niche creators are ignored: Channels with under 100K subscribers rarely receive crowdsourced submissions.
  • ✕ Manual scrub fatigue: You still have to grab the progress bar and hunt for where the real video resumes.
AI SponsorBlock

Automated Zero-Day Detection

The instant you load the page, the extension extracts timestamped captions from YouTube's player and routes them through Groq's high-speed inference engine.

  • ✓ Protected at minute 1: Works the moment the creator hits publish. No human volunteer required.
  • ✓ Shared Supabase Cache: When the first viewer's client analyzes the video, the timestamps are cached globally for all users.
  • ✓ Dual Fallback: Simultaneously checks the SponsorBlock public database in parallel (<150ms).
  • ✓ Self-Correction: Clicking "Undo & Report" submits an instant downvote. 3 consensus downvotes removes bad segments.

System Architecture

How the extension executes under the hood

Engineered as a lightweight Chrome/Edge Manifest V3 extension with zero client-side overhead.

01 / CAPTION EXTRACTION

3-Layer Bridge Architecture

YouTube protects caption endpoints with Proof-of-Origin (&pot=) tokens. Our MAIN world bridge reuses tokens from player network timings, handles regional tracks via &tlang=en, and falls back to Innertube RPCs.

02 / INFERENCE ENGINE

Sub-Second Groq Qwen 3.8

Raw caption cues, description promo links, and chapters are compacted and evaluated by Groq running Qwen 3.8 27B in JSON mode at temperature: 0.1 to extract exact start and end seconds.

03 / DISTRIBUTED CACHING

Supabase Edge Cache

Analyzed segments are saved to PostgreSQL. When you load a video, the extension executes a parallel lookup (<150ms) to Supabase and the SponsorBlock public API. 99% of requests never invoke the AI model.

04 / CONFIDENCE TIERS

Tiered UX Decision Engine

Segments with ≥80% confidence auto-skip instantly with an Undo toast. Segments between 55% and 79% show a manual "Skip Sponsor" prompt so you stay in total control.

05 / CONSENSUS VOTING

Community Self-Healing

Mistakes happen. Clicking "Undo & Report" seeks back to the start and writes a downvote via an atomic PostgreSQL RPC. Segments with ≥3 downvotes and a 2:1 ratio are permanently hidden.

06 / ZERO TRACKING

Local Profile Storage

No telemetry, no analytics beacons, and no accounts. Your watch history never touches our servers. The extension only sends the public video ID and raw subtitle sentences.
